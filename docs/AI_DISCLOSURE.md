# VOXIS LIVE — AI SERVICE & SUB-PROCESSOR DISCLOSURE

**Last Updated:** July 31, 2026

This **AI Service & Sub-Processor Disclosure** ("Disclosure") provides full transparency regarding the artificial intelligence models, cloud services, and on-device machine learning components utilized by **Voxis Live** (desktop application and browser extensions), in compliance with **Article 50 of the European Union Artificial Intelligence Act (EU AI Act)** and global privacy standards.

---

## 1. Transparency & AI Identification Notice (EU AI Act Art. 50)

Voxis Live generates real-time translated audio outputs and synthetic voice dubbing. Pursuant to Article 50 of the EU AI Act:
1. **AI-Generated Synthetic Audio:** The spoken translation you hear is generated by artificial intelligence neural text-to-speech / speech-to-speech models.
2. **Branding & Output Marker:** Voxis includes visual badges ("Powered by Voxis" / "AI Translation") and transcript metadata indicating that content has been generated or manipulated by an AI system.

---

## 2. Cloud AI Sub-Processors & Models Used

Voxis routes real-time audio through enterprise-grade cloud AI APIs for low-latency translation:

| AI Sub-Processor | Primary Model Used | Purpose & Function | Data Terms & Non-Training Policy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Google Cloud / AI Studio** | `gemini-3.5-live-translate-preview` | Real-time speech-to-speech translation, synthetic voice dubbing, live captioning | Audio is processed in-memory transiently over encrypted WebSockets (`wss://`). | **Alibaba Cloud / DashScope** | `qwen3.5-livetranslate-flash-realtime` | Real-time speech-to-speech translation and multilingual voice synthesis | Audio is processed in-memory over encrypted WebSockets (`wss://`). ---

## 3. On-Device (Local) Machine Learning Components

To ensure maximum data privacy, Voxis executes sensitive signal processing and speaker diarization locally on your computer CPU:

1. **Sherpa-ONNX Speaker Diarization:**
   - **Function:** Identifies speaker turn changes (S1 / S2) when multiple individuals speak in sequence.
   - **Privacy Guarantee:** Executed 100% locally on device. Voice embedding vectors are processed in RAM and **never uploaded** to cloud servers or third parties.
2. **Silero VAD (Voice Activity Detection) v5:**
   - **Function:** Filters background noise and music on device CPU prior to cloud transmission.

---

## 4. AI Limitations & Accuracy Disclaimer

1. **Dynamic Translation Nature:** AI translation models operate probabilistically. Translations may contain unexpected pauses, dialectal inaccuracies, or contextual misinterpretations.
2. **Hallucination Warning:** In noisy audio environments or overlapping speech, AI models may hallucinate words not present in the original stream.
3. **Restricted Use Cases:** Voxis is **NOT** designed or certified for legal proceedings, medical diagnosis, emergency services, or safety-critical operations.
